Helena is a DPhil candidate in Philosophy at University of Oxford, funded by the [Institute for Ethics in AI](https://www.oxford-aiethics.ox.ac.uk/). Prior to this, she completed an MPhil in Philosophical Studies under a Jacobsen Scholarship and BA in Philosophy, both at University College London.

Her DPhil thesis gives an account of the nature and value of intimate privacy; the distinctive wrongs or harms that follow from intimate privacy violations; and the relationship between intimate privacy and artificial intelligence (AI). Her project is supervised by Dr Carissa Véliz and Professor Thomas Douglas.

Outside of her academic work, Helena is the lead editor of an [AI Ethics digital magazine](https://www.ethicalintelligence.co/equation), and a researcher for [AI For Good Asia](https://www.aiforgood.asia/team).
